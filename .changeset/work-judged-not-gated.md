Work orders hand judgement to the grading model instead of a fixed rule or a person. Under `independent` and `self` grading, a change outside `scope` no longer fails the grade by itself: it becomes the standing criterion `scope-changes-necessary`; flagged test changes become `test-changes-justified`, judged against the diff with any acknowledgement notes as context; and an agent's amendment that rewrites a criterion, widens `scope`, or drops a non-goal or rewords the objective is recorded with its before and after and judged at the next grade as `amendments-hold-bar`, whose failure makes the next amendment a person's. Under `self` the `--note` must name each such path. `checks` grading keeps the hard rules, and now also needs a person for an agent's scope widening. Removing a criterion or gate always needs a person. `[work.gates] acknowledge` now defaults to `agent`, so a cockpit that left it unset reports the change as a policy weakening at its next sync. `work recover --apply` is open to agents: it checks the lock's recorded host and the containers cockpit labels before removing a lock, refuses when it cannot show the holder gone, and records what it checked in the order's record. The attempt-cap refusal names who closes or amends the order as `[work.gates]` says.
